Entering the water

How this works

A quiet walk across a pond, all drawn live by code. Scroll to move forward; everything else is optional.

What you’re seeing

  • The pond. A 3D scene (three.js). Scrolling moves the camera along a path, so you walk at the pace you scroll.
  • The paintings. Eleven original studies, each made of thousands of random brush strokes generated in code. None copy a real artwork. Each has a short story when you open it.
  • The sky. One day-night cycle spread over the walk, so the light changes as you go. The Time slider lets you set it yourself.
  • The life. Lilies open as you near them, frogs hop between pads, koi swim, birds cross by day, and stars and a moon appear at night.

Things to find

Seven golden lilies ✦ are hidden along the path, and one rare pale koi swims the whole length. Click them. Your progress is saved in this browser only. Stuck? Menu → Hint (or press K) tells you roughly where to look.

Good to know

  • Sound starts off. Turn it on from the button at bottom right; the chord shifts key at each painting.
  • “Add your art” (or dropping images on the page) hangs your own pictures in the frames. They stay on your device and reset when you reload.
  • If it feels slow, try Calm mode. On phones, Tilt lets you look around by moving the device.
  • Menu holds the extra buttons. Copy link shares your spot in the walk.
